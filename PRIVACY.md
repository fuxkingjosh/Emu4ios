# Emu4ios Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 7, 2026_

Emu4ios does not collect, sell or share any personal data.

- **No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.** Emu4ios has none of these.
- **Your games, saves, save states, themes and stats stay on your device.** If you turn on iCloud Sync, they're also stored in your own private iCloud account, which Apple manages and we can't access.
- **Photos:** Emu4ios only asks to *add* screenshots and gameplay clips to your photo library when you save one. It never reads your photos, except an image you pick yourself as a cover or background.
- **Shared themes** contain only the theme's colors, layout, text and an optional background image you chose. You decide who to send them to.

Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/fuxkingjosh/Emu4ios/issues.
